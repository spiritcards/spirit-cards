## 1. Not investment advice; no guaranteed return

- Nothing in this documentation is **financial, investment, legal, or tax advice**.
- Spirit Cards offers **no APY, no yield, no guaranteed return, and no “payback period.”**
- Anything described as a reward — the staker pool, dividends, referral shares — is a **share of real
  fees the project actually collects**, and that share can be **small or zero**.
- **Points have no monetary value** and confer no claim on anything.
- Card values can fall to **zero**. You may lose all money and cards you put in.

Consult your own professional advisers before making any decision.

---

## 2. Battle is gambling

Battle is a **zero-sum wager between players, plus a rake**. You can lose:

- your staked **ETH**, and
- **lives** of your card, and ultimately
- the **card itself** (burned at 0 lives).

Outcomes depend on elements, skills, and an on-chain roll the accepter cannot predict. There is **no
sure win**. Over time, players as a group pay the rake. Do not wager more than you can afford to lose.

- Smart contracts can contain **bugs or vulnerabilities**, even after review. An internal audit and a

- A **formal external audit** has not yet been completed at the time of writing; plan accordingly.
- The current deployment is a **testnet rehearsal (t8, `chainId 46630`)**; the **mainnet `4663`**
  deployment is **pending**, and mainnet addresses are not published.
- The owner (target: a 2-of-3 Safe) can **change parameters**, including revenue splits, and can
  **pause** the game. Review the on-chain config before acting.
- Network, RPC, wallet, and bridge risks are outside the project's control.

## 4. No token, no securities, no Ponzi

- There is **no ERC-20 token**, **no liquidity pool**, and **no Uniswap listing**. `[proven]`
- Spirit Cards is **not** a security, share, or investment product.
- PvP is **zero-sum between players plus a rake**, so it is **not** a Ponzi/Ponzi-like scheme.
- The staker pool is funded by **real fees** (revenue split + rake share), never by new depositors
